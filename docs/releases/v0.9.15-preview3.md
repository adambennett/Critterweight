## Final addition: Rooftop Canvas

Create > Arena Editor > Base > Rooftop Canvas. A blank 32 x 32 metre rooftop with four open
ledges, configurable from 12 to 64 metres independently in width/depth (Details > Layout).
Place normal objects, move the supplied markers, save a custom arena and use it in regular
matches. The blank template itself is editor-only. No wind or fixed obstacles are added.
Resizing preserves your objects, supports Undo/Redo and flags outside items. Cameras and
position fields accommodate the larger platform. Existing ringout damage/return/rules apply.
