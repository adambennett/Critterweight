# Critterweight 0.9.17

This preview overhauls the commentary booth, gives the whole playable roster a new look with
expressions and moving parts, and redraws every fighter's abilities. Combat itself is unchanged:
timing, damage, reach, collision bodies, physics and stats are exactly as in 0.9.16.

## Highlights

- **Commentary that follows the fight.** The booth now decides what to talk about from what is
  actually happening: knockouts, referee stoppages, judges' decisions and round results each have
  their own calls, every fighter's abilities are called by name (and whether they landed), and quiet
  stretches get observations about pressure, momentum, the clock or a fighter who is hurt before
  any joke. Calls expire if they would arrive late, the big moment always wins, and nothing is said
  that the fight contradicts (no ropes or corner crews in a cage, no fence jokes without a fence).
- **Far less repetition.** The booth remembers what you have heard across rematches, Career bouts
  and game restarts, prefers lines you have not heard, and spaces out running jokes.
- **337 new commentary lines and 7 corrected ones**, recorded with the same three voices in 521
  clips, including whole-sentence takes with the roster's names and ceremonial winner calls; after a
  technical check of every clip, 19 were re-recorded where the audio was cut off, flat, rushed or
  paused oddly. 59 older lines that got facts wrong were retired. Speech still works fully offline.
- **New art for all eight fighters.** Stoke, Dredge, Gusto, Dozer, Slurry, Jitters, Varga and Knell
  were rebuilt with new forms, faces and details, readable from the fight camera: Stoke's riveted
  boiler and back stacks, Dredge's barnacled plates and mooring chain, Slurry's fin and glowing
  pores, Jitters' battery-jelly bell and tentacles, Knell's haunted diving suit and lantern, Varga's
  new fighter build, and more. Eyes look at the opponent and blink, brows and jaws react to the
  fight, and crests, fins, chains, tentacles, hoses and hair swing with the motion. A new finish
  gives cloth, metal, shells, jelly and ghostly surfaces their own look.
- **Legacy Varga skin** (Settings › Graphics): brings back Varga's original look from before this
  update. It is your own preference: it applies everywhere you see him (fighter select, matches,
  practice, Career, spectating, online, the hub), other players see their own choice, and he fights
  exactly the same either way.
- **New ability effects for every fighter.** Abilities, JUICE, charged heavies, projectiles, ground
  effects, elemental hits and reactions now have a build-up, a strike, motion and a fade, in each
  fighter's style: Stoke's steam jets and furnace blasts, Dredge's tides and whirlpools, Gusto's
  vortices, Dozer's dust and rock spires, Slurry's sludge, Jitters' lightning, Knell's ghostly bell
  and lantern, and Varga's crisp gold impacts. Every area effect now shows its true shape and
  reach on the floor (a cone is drawn as that cone), the effects read on bright and dark floors,
  and they respect the Effects quality and Reduce flashing settings. The hub shows your fighter's
  ability effects too.

## Verification

The commentary was audited before and after the overhaul: the same twelve seeded bouts and two
scripted sequences, played in real time across a game restart on 0.9.16 and on this build. The booth
used 338 different lines instead of 125, exact repeats within a bout fell from 137 to 15 (and from 106
to 7 across rematches), the referee stoppage was called as a stoppage rather than a knockout, and it
talked slightly more often than before (10.8 lines a minute instead of 9.5). The new art and effects
were captured and compared shot by shot against 0.9.16, and saves from 0.9.16 load and continue. Nothing here has been listened to or
playtested by a person yet, so please report anything that sounds or looks off.
