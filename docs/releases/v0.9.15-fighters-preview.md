# v0.9.15 fighters preview

Local work on `feature/v0.9.15-fighters`, based on the v0.9.15 preview 3. No release publication or graphical launch is requested.

## Added fighters

- **Varga, Scrap:** Jeremy's original Cage Rules fighter model, four limb buttons, held strike modifiers, stance switching, checks, low guard and opponent-relative movement. Cut the Angle, Second Wind and Championship Rounds supply the native ability/JUICE slots. The native clinch/pummel/throw system adapts the original grapple input; Cage Rules ground positions and submissions are not part of this port.
- **Knell, Spectral:** an original haunted diving suit, regular control layout, Ghost Light projectile, Through the Veil phase escape and Last Bell pulses/protection.
- Both fighters are playable in the standard selection flows, Random, campaign and AI bouts; both have Compendium descriptions, controller glyphs, three campaign build directions and new elemental interactions. The Full Card achievement description follows the expanded roster.
- Network protocol 6 adds the extra input bits/counters. Older protocol versions are rejected at connection. Online opponent-relative movement respects the server-selected target.
- Varga's tutorial explains his actual inputs and avoids charged-heavy/dash-combo requirements that do not exist in his layout. Tutorial companion references no longer retain a finished course.

## Controls and sources

Full guides: [Varga](../kits/f_varga.md) and [Knell](../kits/f_knell.md). The in-game fighter sheet contains the same actions and current binding glyphs. Source provenance and rebuild commands: `tools/cage_rules/README.md`; original Knell art definition: `tools/forge/defs/creatures/f_knell.js`.
