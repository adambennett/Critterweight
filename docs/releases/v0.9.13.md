# Critterweight 0.9.13

This preview adds free, licensed audio replacements and makes Random available anywhere a fighter is selected.

## Audio

- Replaces title/poster music with Funkorama, Cannery radio with Backbay Lounge, both combat slots with Furious Freak, and boss music with Volatile Reaction. The finished recordings use no mismatched generated stems; boss JUICE still jumps to section B.
- Adds Kenney CC0 result jingles, punch/impact and guard sounds, surface footsteps, menu feedback, and book/coin UI cues.
- Adds CC0 crowd ambience/applause and quiet dock water. Freesound crowd clips were skipped because their download requires an account; a public CC0 OpenGameArt source was used instead.
- No paid version is included. Kevin MacLeod tracks are CC BY 4.0 and require the attribution in Credits; the other installed clips are CC0 1.0. Full provenance and SHA-256 list: docs/audio/replacement-audio-inventory.txt.
- Named creature calls and custom announcer/coach lines remain generated so their identities and written performances stay intact.
- The imported bank passes Godot import, runtime validation and asset-hash checks. Please audition the music in-game; automated checks confirm technical integration, not sound quality.

## Fighter selection

Every fighter picker now includes Random, including campaign/Fight Camp, Bookie, versus, multiplayer lobby, AI Lab, Cannery sparring and the locker. A random choice resolves to a concrete fighter before a fight or campaign run is created.
