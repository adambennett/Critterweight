# Critterweight 0.9.12

Fixes and additions from the 0.9.11 playtest.

## Campaigns and watching fights

- Escape and the Back prompt leave the campaign lobby correctly.
- A completed campaign fight opens its reward/next decision and saves that state. Resume restores the pending reward, event, shop, rest or route map. An unfinished fight still restarts when resumed.
- If a v0.9.11 save still marks the first fight as unfinished, it will replay that fight once: the old bug never saved its completed result. Subsequent wins advance and save normally.
- Controller navigation connects the suspended-run buttons to the Bookie's Table and the new-run form.
- The Bookie's Table shows the live 3D fights, with 1–4x playback. Skip ahead runs the same fight at 8x; Watch returns to it immediately.
- Spectate cycles through Follow, Locked and Free cameras. Free flight uses LS/WASD to move, RS/right-drag to look, A/Space to rise and LT/Shift to descend. The Bookie's free view uses View/Tab to switch between flying and the table controls.

## Menus, Compendium and controls

- The main menu keeps its poster design with eight groups and no scrolling. Tutorial is under Fight; AI Lab and Mods are under Create; Controls and Credits are under Settings.
- Submenus stay open when the mouse moves toward their options or away from the left column.
- New Compendium: inspect the fighters' actual 3D models, stats, abilities and cooldowns; browse elements and reactions, arenas, game rules, field objects and a practical guide.
- Right-stick scrolling is shared across focused information panels, text and lists. Focused rows scroll far enough to show their bottom outline.
- The new-rules picker uses a grid that fits the screen.
- Arena Editor map focus keeps the sticks controlling the map. View switches back to the panels; Palette and Details can each be hidden. Toolbar and control hints wrap cleanly, including after an input-device switch.
- Dialogue uses your profile name, with Rookie replacing the old default You. Rename it in Profile.
- Ability names now accompany their HUD bindings. RB works for all six playable fighters: many second abilities are a buff, counter, hover, cloud, dive or placed pad rather than a projectile. The Compendium explains each one.

## The Cannery

- Chum Cannon offers Warm-up, Pressure and Homing hell. Harder settings fire faster, predict movement and, at the highest level, track early in flight while leaving a final dodge window.
- Crate Toss calls for near/middle/far targets with bonuses and streaks. Throw strength matters; one repeated full heave is no longer the best strategy. Used crates cannot score twice, and all crates reset after the game.
- Walls block crate pickups. Movement direction follows the visible camera throughout room transitions.
- Needles' price board clearly identifies itself as a preview. It explains that purchases and services are available at campaign Shop nodes, what each service does, and the difference between Scrip and Clout.

## Settings and audio

- Resolution is a dropdown: select the desired mode once, then Keep/Revert once.
- Show FPS displays a counter above menus and fights.
- Opening an AI Lab dropdown keeps the music playing; owned popup windows count as application focus.
- Removed continuous buzz/hum layers from eight ambience loops and repaired overlapping ambience fades.
- FSR options now say they work across GPU vendors, including NVIDIA. Godot 4.5.1's stock renderer does not expose DLSS, so this release does not add a DLSS option.
- [Complete generated-audio inventory and stock replacement shortlist](https://github.com/adambennett/PokeMMA/blob/v0.9.12/docs/audio/REPLACEMENT_GUIDE.md), with individual filenames, source links and license notes. Proposed stock assets are not installed in this release.
