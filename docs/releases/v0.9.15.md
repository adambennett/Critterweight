# Critterweight 0.9.15

This combined preview release brings together the v0.9.15 playtest work, including the campaign and editor improvements, new fighters, expanded recorded commentary, and the community soundtrack additions.

## Highlights

- Adds Varga, a Fighting-style martial artist with a deeper move set and controller support, and Knell, a Spectral fighter with a standard control scheme. Both have abilities, campaign upgrades, and Compendium entries.
- Adds the editor-only Rooftop Canvas arena layout, letting custom arenas use a configurable square or rectangular floor bounded by rooftop edges.
- Improves campaign progression and run resumption, route-map presentation and event art, combat announcements and fight HUD, controller navigation, arena editing, and fighter selection.
- Adds 854 new Devon/Kendall announcer takes, alongside the recorded coach dialogue and player-name variants, for more varied calls and longer booth conversations.
- Adds eight community music tracks and a Jukebox for choosing ordered, shuffled, or repeating playlists. Audio credits and license details are included in-game and in the bundled documentation.
- Includes the previously playtested v0.9.15 fixes, including the saved monitor choice, quieter single menu-navigation sound, patch notes, inverted spectator camera option, and controller-friendly menus.

## Verification

The combined source suite completed 775 tests: 774 passed, zero failed, and one graphical-audio test was skipped because the suite ran without the graphical audio backend. The packaged combined preview completed 72 checks: 70 passed, zero failed, and two source-only checks were skipped. All 406 scripts compiled successfully.

The packaged checks cover the recorded speech and music bank, announcer behavior, both new fighters, the v0.9.15 regressions, arena canvas, UI focus, and layout. The release packaging script also runs a fresh script check and boots the tagged Windows game to the title screen. The automated suite confirms technical integration; please continue to report any issues found during play.
