# Critterweight 0.9.16

This preview adds a persistent fighter Career recreated from Cage Rules' career, optional 10-point must scoring and a referee for the standard duel, and a built-in Cage Rules rules preset. Critterweight's own combat stays as it is: there is no grappling, ground game or submissions, and grabs keep their simple throw-and-escape layer.

## Highlights

- **Career** (Campaign › Career, now first in the Campaign menu): build one fighter over many bouts. Pick any playable fighter, a ring name, a nickname and a difficulty, then train one of six stats each week (Stamina, Chin, Speed, Power, Clinch, Defence) or rest to heal. Each week brings two or three fight offers from nine promotions in four tiers, each showing the opponent's record and rating, a danger tag, the purse and the win bonus. Wins raise popularity and open bigger promotions; at the top a five-round title shot waits, and champions defend the belt. Purses buy gym upgrades, and injuries heal over the weeks. Records, standing, fight history and a news log are kept for every career. Three save slots per profile save after every action, and Retire moves a save aside instead of deleting it.
- **10-point scoring** (Rules Editor › Scoring): fighters cannot be knocked out by damage, so the HP bars disappear. Every round is fought, and three judges (Even card, Volume, Impact) score each one 10-9, 10-10 or 10-8 from damage, clean strikes, knockdowns, throws, ring-outs, parries and guard breaks. The HUD shows the round and each judge's running total, a scorecard appears between rounds, and the results screen shows every card. A drawn decision follows the Sudden death setting.
- **Referee** (Rules Editor › Referee, works with either scoring): nobody can hit a fighter who is down. The referee counts strike knockdowns, waves a fighter up after four seconds (you can get up sooner, and staying down recovers stamina faster) and stops the fight after five knockdowns in one round. Jeremy's original Cage Rules referee is imported through the Cage Rules asset tools: he keeps his distance during the action, holds the attacker back, waves off stoppages and raises the winner's arm. Online players see the same referee.
- **Cage Rules preset**: the Standard Duel with 10-point scoring and the referee on, available wherever rules presets are listed. Career bouts use it with three rounds (five for title fights) and allow draws.
- The in-game patch notes now include v0.9.15, which the previous build was missing.

## Verification

The full source suite on the release commit completed 862 tests: 861 passed, zero failed, and one graphical-audio test was skipped because the suite runs without the graphical audio backend. All 437 scripts compile. Against the exported Windows game, 96 headless and 26 rendered tests covering 10-point scoring, the referee, the Career, rules presets, snapshots and recorded speech passed with no skips. The release script also runs a fresh script check, boots the game to the title screen and passes the 20 rendered playtest regressions.

The judges' thresholds, the referee's five-knockdown limit and the Career's injury and fight-of-the-night numbers were calibrated on 52 headless bot duels (DECISIONS D-045). Online behaviour was checked over loopback only, and no physical-controller or human playtest has been done yet, so please report anything that feels off in play, especially the judging of close rounds and the Career economy.
